## Disclosure Policy

All findings are reported to vendors before publication. I follow a 90-day
disclosure timeline from first vendor contact. Technical details are not
published until the embargo date regardless of vendor response.

Advisories are published here on their respective disclosure dates to provide
reference URLs for CVE records.

---

## Methodology

All testing is performed on local instances only. No production systems, real
credentials, or live cloud endpoints are targeted. Synthetic tokens and local
listeners are used to confirm vulnerability primitives.
